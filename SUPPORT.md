# Support

This is an independent, unofficial research project maintained on a volunteer
basis. There is no commercial support, no service-level agreement, and no
guarantee of a response.

## Before asking for help

1. **Read `README.md`.** It states the scope of the project, what it is and is
   not, and how to run it.
2. **Read the status section** (in `README.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`). It records
   the current development status accurately, including what has *not* been
   demonstrated. Many questions are answered there — in particular, questions
   about whether the project "works" yet.
3. **Search existing issues**, both open and closed. Your question may already
   have an answer, and a closed issue may explain why something is out of scope.

## Asking a question

If the above does not answer it, open an issue using the form that fits:

* **Bug report** — something in the tooling is broken or behaves incorrectly.
* **Research validation** — you have a result, a reproduction, or a check of a
  claim, and want it reviewed or recorded.
* **Hardware test result** — you ran something on physical hardware and want to
  report what happened. Read the hardware safety policy in `README.md` first,
  and do not run physical experiments you have not been authorized to run.
* **Feature request** — you want a capability that does not exist.
* **Documentation** — a document is wrong, unclear, or missing something.

Blank issues are disabled so that reports arrive with the information needed to
act on them. General questions about the project that do not fit a form can be
raised via the discussions or contact link configured for the repository.

## What this project cannot help with

The project cannot and will not help with any of the following. Requests for
them will be closed.

* **Game support, troubleshooting, or configuration.** The project does not
  support any game, does not provide game fixes, and cannot help you get a
  particular title running.
* **Anti-cheat questions, bypasses, or compatibility.** Nothing here is
  intended to interact with, evade, or modify anti-cheat systems, and the
  project will not assist with anything in that area.
* **Piracy, or obtaining proprietary assets.** The project will not help locate,
  download, extract, or redistribute game binaries, game data, or any other
  proprietary content, and does not accept such material as contributions.
* **Running proprietary assets on your behalf.** The project does not run
  third-party proprietary content and cannot validate results that depend on it.
* **Hardware modification, overclocking, power or voltage tuning, or driver
  patching.** The project's safety policy forbids these, and support requests
  asking for them are out of scope.
* **Legal advice** on the status of any third-party software or mod.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by NVIDIA, AMD,
Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, or any third-party mod or project
referenced by the research. Support questions directed at those parties will not
